Chapter 02
Playbooks

How we would play it.

Six situations we are set up for, and how we would handle each one. These are approaches rather than case studies — real numbers from real accounts go in Chapter 03 once there are accounts to report on.

Meta

Offer testing for e-commerce

Several different angles running against one offer, with the budget drifting toward whatever hits a cost per customer you can actually afford. Not whatever has the prettiest number on the dashboard.

Angle → offer → reallocate

TikTok

Spark Ads from owned content

Your best organic posts and creator videos promoted exactly as they are, so the likes and comments already on them come along for the ride instead of being thrown away at upload.

Organic → whitelist → scale

Creative

Angle mining from reviews

We read your reviews, support tickets and comments end to end. People tell you exactly why they bought, in their own words. Those words become the first three seconds of an ad.

Research → brief → batch

Meta

Rescue on a plateaued account

For when spend is flat and the cost per customer is quietly creeping up. Pull the structure apart, check the tracking is telling the truth, then run one clean round of new ads before anyone touches the budget.

Audit → rebuild → one cycle

TikTok

First month on TikTok

For brands doing fine on Meta with nothing on TikTok. Proper native creative built from scratch, a deliberately small budget, and an honest answer at the end about whether the channel suits what you sell.

Native build → test → verdict

Creative

Winner iteration

When one ad is carrying the whole account. New openings on the same footage, new lengths, new formats, so there is a replacement ready before it burns out rather than three weeks after.

Isolate → vary → replace

Everything we do sits in one of these five steps. Nothing happens off the loop, and the weekly read is the thing you hold us to.

Account, tracking, margins and everything you have run so far. We agree the one number that decides whether an ad lives or dies, and write down what the last 90 days actually did.

Chapter 06
Fit

Read the right-hand column first.

We would rather lose the call than take money for an account we cannot move.

Good fit

  • +Already spending on Meta or TikTok, with a few months of history behind you.
  • +Repeat purchase or a decent margin, so there is room for this to work.
  • +Someone in-house who can approve creative in days rather than weeks.
  • +Happy to be judged on what a new customer actually costs you, not on dashboard numbers.
  • +Comfortable putting a lot of ads out, some of which will not flatter the brand book.

Not a fit

  • −You want a media buyer to run the account on the ads you already have.
  • −Creative sign-off goes through a committee.
  • −You want a guaranteed return.
  • −There is no purchase tracking, and no appetite to fix it first.
  • −You sell something neither platform will let us advertise.

Chapter 08
Questions

Asked on every first call.

Q1Who owns the ad accounts and the creative?+

You do. We work inside your Business Manager and TikTok account through partner access you can revoke in a click, and everything we make is licensed to you. If we stop working together you keep the accounts, the data and the ads, and nothing needs moving anywhere.

Q2What do you need from us to start?+

Access to the ad accounts, your analytics and your store back end, an honest number for your margins, and one person who can approve creative. Week one is the audit, so the first thing you get back is a written read on where you are rather than a pile of new ads.

Q3Is there a minimum spend?+

Yes. Below a certain monthly spend there simply is not enough data per test to learn anything, and you would be paying us to guess.

Fill inYour minimum monthly ad spend, and the minimum per-platform budget you require.
Q4Do you guarantee a ROAS target?+

No, and we would be wary of anyone who does. What we will commit to is the number that decides whether an ad lives, how much new creative goes out each week, a set launch day, and numbers you can check yourself.

Q5Do you make the creative, or just run media?+

Either. Managed growth means we run the media and brief whoever makes your ads. Creative engine means we make them too. Most accounts that have stalled are short of ads, not short of media management.

Q6How quickly do you launch?+

Audit first, then the first batch goes live on the next launch day, once we have confirmed the tracking is telling the truth. Going live before that is how an account spends three weeks learning nothing.

Fill inYour real onboarding timeline in weeks, from signed to first batch live.
Q7How do we talk day to day?+

A shared channel during the week, a written update every week, and a longer call once a month. No standing meeting that exists purely to be a meeting.
